# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Disagreement about scanner behavior is expected;
harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and disclosure of another person's private
information are not accepted.

Assume good faith while discussing intent, but evaluate security claims from evidence.
Avoid posting live credentials, private source, or uncoordinated exploit details in public
issues.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's contact
channel. Enforcement decisions prioritize participant safety and the security of affected
users.
